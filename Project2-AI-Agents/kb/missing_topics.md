# Topics Deliberately Missing from NorthPeak Outfitters Knowledge Base

These topics are deliberately omitted from the store policies. Under Rule **ES7**, any customer inquiry regarding these topics must trigger **`REFUSE_NO_POLICY`**; the system must state that the policy cannot be confirmed, create a human follow-up task, and never invent rules:

- Gift cards or vouchers
- Loyalty, rewards, or points programs
- Price matching or competitor price guarantees
- Goodwill compensation beyond standard shipping-fee refunds
- Business, wholesale, or B2B bulk orders
- Technical compatibility with other brands' equipment
- Legal advice or liability settlements
